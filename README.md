## About

I'm a CS graduate from Kraków fascinated by the overlap between software and design — how thoughtful interfaces and visualizations can make technology feel natural and meaningful. My thesis explored AI-generated image detection using transformer architectures (ViT-B/16 and CLIP), giving me a deep dive into computer vision and ML evaluation.

Long-term, I want to build products that sit at the hardware-software boundary and genuinely change how people interact with machines. Until then: shipping code, learning fast, and collaborating with people who care about craft.

Currently open to opportunities in **web development**, **data science**, and **UX-adjacent engineering roles**.
